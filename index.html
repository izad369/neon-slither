How Online Play Works

Every player's phone only sends the direction it wants to move. One central server keeps the real, official copy of the whole arena — every snake's position, every piece of food, every collision.

The server constantly sends back "here's what the arena actually looks like right now" to everyone, so all players see the same shared world at the same time — no matter how many people are playing.

That's also why an internet connection is needed for online play: your phone is talking to that server the whole time. Offline mode skips the server entirely and plays against bots on your own device instead.
